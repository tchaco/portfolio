DOMINO’S / WELCOME PACK

DESIGN , ILLUSTRATION

To ensure that new employees feel valued and prepared from day one, Domino’s has put together a comprehensive Welcome Pack with a distinctive package. This kit includes essential items such as uniforms and branded hats, helping new team members look the part and feel like they belong. In addition to apparel, the kit also contains important guidelines and company policies, providing clear expectations and helpful tips for success. With the delivery of these tools to the new employees, Domino's aims to make onboarding effortless as they begin their journey with the company.
